:: SO WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS PLACE? ::

New Baltimore is a waterfront city in Macomb County, sitting on Anchor Bay / Lake St. Clair. It's suburban Detroit without feeling like you're in the middle of Detroit — old downtown, lake water, M-29, neighborhoods, parking lots, docks, and everybody somehow ending up at the same places.

In 2015, you could go downtown, hit the lake, get food, sit in somebody's car, wander around Burke Park, or just drive until somebody finally decided they were hungry. ♡ Nobody needed a plan.
