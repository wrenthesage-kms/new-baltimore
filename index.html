Important: when somebody says a road name, they're not just giving you random directions. Those names tell you what part of the area you're in.

M-29 means lakeshore. Washington and Front mean downtown. Burke Park means water. 23 and 24 Mile mean the bigger suburban/commercial shit. I-94 means you're actually going somewhere.

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE / 001

THE ROADS

because “we're on M-29” is supposed to mean something

M-29

the lake road

M-29 is the lakeshore route through the area. It's two-lane asphalt, roadside businesses, storefronts, residential driveways, streetlights, and random glimpses of the lake.

It's also the road for driving around when nobody actually wants to go home yet.

at night: headlights + storefront glow + lake between trees.
WASHINGTON

downtown downtown

Washington Street is the main commercial spine through downtown. Older buildings sit close to the sidewalk, with local businesses and residences packed into the same area.

This is where you're going to see somebody you know while you're literally just trying to do an errand.

FRONT

waterfront side

Front Street runs along the waterfront side of downtown. More open than Washington, more views of Anchor Bay, more boats, and in summer, way more people wandering around.

23 / 24

suburban shit

23 Mile and 24 Mile are where things start feeling more like the broader suburban Metro Detroit area: wide roads, parking lots, strip malls, fast food, corporate signs, planned neighborhoods, etc.

Less “walk downtown and accidentally know everybody.” More “get in the car.”

I-94

the actual highway

I-94 is the major interstate connection. It ties New Baltimore into the wider Detroit / suburban / Port Huron road network.

Multi-lane highway. Concrete. Barriers. Overhead signs. Billboards. Headlights. Traffic. The complete opposite of casually cruising M-29.

YES THIS MATTERS

THE LAKE

Anchor Bay is the northern reach of Lake St. Clair.

There's water, docks, boats, fishing, swimming, waterfront parks, lake weather, and people actually spending time outside because the lake is right there.

In summer, the smell is lake water, sunscreen, cut grass, and food cooking somewhere nearby.

Wind comes off the water. Summer storms change shit. Wet pavement smells different. Boats are normal. The lake is just part of the environment.

BURKE
PARK

Walter & Mary Burke Park is the big public waterfront hangout in town.

There's a beach, swimming, fishing, a long dock, boardwalk, benches, picnic areas, pavilion, playground, and the old red water tower.

Downtown is basically right behind it, so you can go from streets and storefronts to the water without leaving town.

BAY-RAMA FISHFLY FESTIVAL

Five days in late June where downtown and the waterfront get absolutely taken over by the festival.

Carnival rides. Festival tents. Live music. Food stalls. Games. Community groups. Families. Teenagers. Volunteers. Local businesses.

There's a parade through the streets and fireworks over Anchor Bay near Burke Park.

everybody is outside. everybody.

2015 SPECIFIC

FESTIVAL PARK

In 2015 this isn't some finished, polished park dropped into the middle of town.

It's a developing recreational area: grassy fields, gravel, portable bleachers, chain-link around baseball diamonds, construction energy, hot asphalt, dirt, bats, whistles, cut grass.

Youth sports and families are around. It's also quieter and less crowded than the waterfront.
