01 THE PLACE

THIS IS
NEW BALTIMORE.

It's small enough that everybody knows somebody. Big enough that you can disappear for a few hours.

New Baltimore sits on Anchor Bay at the northern edge of Lake St. Clair, caught somewhere between old waterfront town and suburban Metro Detroit.

There are streets you know by name, places you don't think twice about, people you keep running into, and stretches of road where nobody asks where you're going.

Most of the time, there isn't anywhere you need to be.

Yeah, there's a school. Yeah, everybody is proud of the Tars. No, school isn't the entire universe.

ABCC is one of the biggest social collision points in the area. Classes are small. People know each other. Somebody's always walking somewhere with somebody else.
