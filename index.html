THE ROADS

01

M-29

Lakeshore route. Slow, familiar, kind of scenic, kind of unpolished. Businesses, houses, driveways, lake glimpses, streetlights.

translation: the road you take when nobody wants to go home yet.
02

WASHINGTON ST.

Downtown spine. Older buildings, local businesses, sidewalks, people you recognize.

translation: you are never anonymous here.
03

FRONT ST.

Runs along the waterfront. More open, more lake, more boats. Easy place to go from downtown to sitting around doing absolutely nothing.

04

23 / 24 MILE

Wider roads, parking lots, strip malls, chain stores, fast food, traffic. The outer suburban ring.

translation: welcome to the part where everything requires a car.

I-94

Major highway. Detroit, Port Huron, suburban Michigan, getting out, getting back.
