THE SHORT VERSION

where the hell are we?

new baltimore sits on anchor bay in macomb county, close enough to metro detroit that nobody here thinks of it as some isolated little lake town.

it's waterfront suburbs, old downtown buildings, residential streets, marinas, parks, school parking lots, strip malls, m-29, i-94, and everybody running into somebody they know.

ABCC is right in the middle of that social web. classes are small. people recognize each other. professors know names. students see each other downtown, at the lake, at restaurants, at parties, in parking lots, and sitting in somebody else's car for absolutely no reason.

nothing needs to become a huge event. most things are just something to do.

★ important

this is 2015.

snapchat. instagram. twitter. tumblr. vine. kik.

ANCHOR BAY COMMUNITY COLLEGE

ABCC

ABCC is not some enormous university campus. it's a small community college woven directly into the surrounding town.

classes are small enough that people know where everyone sits. around thirty students is normal. desks. actual classrooms. professors who recognize students outside class.

the Tars are the school identity. red and white are everywhere.

THE TOWN

new baltimore

lakefront suburb. old downtown. metro detroit orbit.

new baltimore is small enough for people to recognize each other and connected enough that nobody treats it like the middle of nowhere.

the waterfront shapes the town without controlling every second of it. sometimes the lake is the whole point. sometimes you're just driving through town with the windows down.
