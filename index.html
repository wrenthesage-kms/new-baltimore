OKAY. WHAT THE HELL IS NEW BALTIMORE?

SMALL TOWN.
LAKE TOWN.
DETROIT ORBIT.

New Baltimore is the kind of place where you can leave Anchor Bay Community College, get in somebody's car, drive down Washington, end up at Burke Park, see three people you know, buy a pop, and somehow wind up sitting on a dock two hours later.

It isn't rural. It isn't some isolated little village. It's a residential waterfront community in Macomb County, close enough to the rest of Metro Detroit that you can disappear onto I-94 whenever you want.

But inside town? Everybody overlaps.

THE THING THAT IS ALWAYS THERE

ANCHOR BAY

The northern reach of Lake St. Clair.

You don't have to be doing anything special to notice it. There's water. Boats. Wind. Wet pavement after storms. Grass. Sunscreen. Food cooking somewhere.

Sometimes the bay is the reason everyone is outside. Sometimes it's just sitting behind the conversation while somebody argues about where to eat.

FISHFLY SEASON

POP POP POP POP POP

Imagine driving down the road and the asphalt is covered in fishflies.

You don't panic. You don't stop. You lower the windows.
